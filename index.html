About

I am a researcher working on reinforcement learning and control theory, with applications in power and energy systems. I am currently an Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellow at the AI for Science Institute and Duffield College of Engineering, Cornell University, working with Fengqi You. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences at Tsinghua University in 2025, advised by Chenye Wu and Ran Duan. From 2023 to 2024, I was a visiting student researcher in Computing and Mathematical Sciences at Caltech, working with Adam Wierman. I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Software Engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2020.

Research

My research develops theory and algorithms for understanding and exploiting the value of information and structure in sequential decision-making. I study imperfect predictions, decomposable dynamics, Bellman convexity, Lyapunov stability, and chance constraints, with an emphasis on sample-efficient reinforcement learning and rigorous performance guarantees.

My applied work addresses the reliable operation of power and energy systems and the integration of AI data centers with the grid.
