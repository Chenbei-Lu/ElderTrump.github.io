About me

I am an Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI Postdoctoral Fellow at the AI for Science Institute, Cornell University, working with Prof. Fengqi You.

Before joining Cornell, I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences at Tsinghua University in 2025, advised by Prof. Chenye Wu and Prof. Ran Duan. I was also a visiting student researcher in Computing and Mathematical Sciences at Caltech from 2023 to 2024, advised by Prof. Adam Wierman. I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Software Engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2020.

Research areas

Research

My research develops theoretical and algorithmic foundations for reliable sequential decision-making under uncertainty, with applications in energy systems and AI infrastructure.

01

Control and learning under uncertainty

Reinforcement learning, stochastic optimization, and control methods that use structure such as predictions, factorization, and constraints.

02

Energy systems and market operations

Storage control, economic dispatch, unit commitment, demand flexibility, and privacy-aware coordination for power-system operation.

03

AI infrastructure and data centers

Optimization models for power supply, workload flexibility, and regional infrastructure planning for AI data centers.
